How measurements work

Horizontal ground metres, not slope-adjusted distances. Tee distance is to your marked green centre. Hazard reach is the nearest boundary; carry is the farthest boundary distance, a conservative radius—not a shot-line recommendation. OB shows nearest boundary only.

Fairway width is measured perpendicular to the straight selected-tee → green-centre axis, at each station. Separate fairway intervals are shown separately. Green dimensions are extents along/across that axis. Live front/back are intersections of your position → green-centre line with the green edge. Drawing and GPS accuracy limit real-world accuracy.
